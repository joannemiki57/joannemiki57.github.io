I'm applying to Ph.D. programs for Fall 2027. I'll finish my M.S. at UW–Madison in May 2027. Research interests: multimodal data analysis, machine learning, in-situ process monitoring, and federated learning for manufacturing. CV · email

I'm a master's student in Industrial & Systems Engineering at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, advised by Prof. Hantang Qin. I've been a research assistant in his Flexible Electronics & Additive Printing (FEAP) Lab since fall 2025, when I first came to Madison as a visiting student. In spring 2026 I also did an independent study with Prof. Kaibo Liu on federated learning for remaining-useful-life prediction. This past summer I was a research intern in Prof. Martin Jun's Laboratory for Advanced Multiscale Manufacturing (LAMM) at Purdue University.

I did my B.S. in Industrial Engineering at Ajou University in Suwon, South Korea, where I finished first in my class of 139. At Ajou I worked in Prof. Jooyeon Lee's New Industrial Convergence R&D Lab on solar-panel telemetry and a microfluidic sweat sensor.

My research is machine learning for manufacturing: multimodal data analysis, in-situ process monitoring, and federated learning. Right now I'm most interested in sound: whether a microphone next to a machine can tell us what the process is actually doing, layer by layer. I also work on visual defect inspection and on models that still hold up when the data comes from different machines and labs.

I like to learn new stuff, and my projects show it: inkjet printing, laser powder bed fusion audio, camera calibration, an oral cancer screening app. Manufacturing is where most of them meet. Outside the lab I play violin, build Lego, and keep a long list of movies and books to get through.

Feel free to reach out if you'd like to chat about any of this! Here is my CV.
