  1. Mfg. Letters
    Federated Learning-Based Regression Framework to Predict Printed Line Width in Electrohydrodynamic Inkjet Printing
    Pengyu Zhang, Junghyun Yun, and Hantang Qin
    Manufacturing Letters, vol. 49, pp. 1298–1307. 54th SME North American Manufacturing Research Conference (NAMRC 54), 2026
    DOIProject
    Accurate prediction of printed line width is essential to achieve dimensional precision and process stability in electrohydrodynamic (EHD) printing. However, training a reliable regression model requires large, diverse datasets that capture the full spectrum of process variability. In practice, data generated from different EHD systems or laboratories often exhibit domain-specific characteristics due to differences in environmental factors such as humidity, temperature, and equipment condition. These discrepancies, coupled with data privacy and ownership constraints, hinder the creation of centralized datasets for model development. To address this challenge, this study introduces a federated learning (FL)-based regression framework that enables collaborative model training across distributed EHD systems without exposing raw data. Each client trains a local regressor using its own process and imaging data, and a global model is periodically aggregated to integrate distributed knowledge while preserving confidentiality. Experimental results demonstrate that the developed FL framework effectively captures inter-system variability and achieves superior prediction accuracy compared with individual learning. Furthermore, transfer learning experiments confirm the framework's scalability to new inks and substrates. This work establishes a foundation for privacy-preserving, generalizable, and data-driven control in next-generation additive manufacturing systems.
